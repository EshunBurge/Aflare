    Account

    Privacy, data & about

    Aflare aims to make fire awareness faster.

    Aflare brings NASA satellite fire detections, official incident reports, weather alerts, fire-weather forecasts and air quality together in one place, so you know sooner when fire weather or a fire is near the people and places you care about.

    What Aflare stores

    Your name, email, profile photo, saved locations, emergency contacts and alert settings. With a synced account this is stored in your private Firestore document, readable only by you; otherwise it stays on this device.

    Checklist progress and read articles are stored the same way. Your device's live location is used only when you ask for it (adding your current location, “Current location” on Home, the Call tab) and is not stored unless you save it as a place. To look up data, the app sends the area around a place to the public services below; they see your IP address like any website would.

      Important limits

      A satellite detection is a heat anomaly, not a confirmed wildfire. It can be a prescribed burn, crop fire or industrial heat source, and satellites pass only every few hours, so clouds and timing can hide real fires.

      Reported fires come from US fire agencies via NIFC and can lag behind events. Official warnings come from the US National Weather Service. Aflare does not show evacuation orders — those come from your county or sheriff.

      Fire weather is Aflare's own simple estimate from the forecast. It is not an official fire-danger rating and does not predict whether a fire will start.

      Alerts are checked while Aflare is open (and, on Android with the installed app, sometimes in the background). There is no alert server, so alerts can be late or missed. Aflare never replaces official emergency alerts or a call to emergency services.
